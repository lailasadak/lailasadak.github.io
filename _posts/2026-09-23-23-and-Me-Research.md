Have you ever had a conversation with someone and you start talking about where they are from? Have they ever started giving you different percentages to different countries?If so, they most likely got that information from 23 and Me. 23 and Me is genetic testing company that provides at home DNA kits that are sent back to labs for ancestral analysis. Anne Wojcicki, Linda Avey, and Paul Cusenza all started the company in 2006 but shortly, Avey and Cusenza left after not even 5 years since its establishment. Wojcicki was married to one of Google’s co-founders, Sergey Brin, which aided in the initial investment. Google invested 3.9 million which covered almost half of the total investment of 9 million by 2007.  Genentech, New Enterprise Associates, and Mohr Davidow Ventures covered the rest. In May of 2025, 23 and Me filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and is currently sitting at a revenue of 170-175 million with it continuing to decline. 23 and Me’s main focus was for people to have accessible genetic testing that can explain where your history traces back to, genetic health risks, and different traits by using saliva based SNP (single nucleotide polymorphism) genotyping. In 2013, just after 7 years of building up the company, 23 and Me received a cease and desist letter to start down all operations. Wojcicki battled in court, submitting over 7,00 pages to the FDA and won the case 2 years later. It did really well until 2023, it got hacked and exposed the personal information of 7 million people. The board quit, stock went down a whopping 98%, and there was really no other choice but to file for bankruptcy. Now, 23 and Me is a non profit organization under the TTAM Research Institute. Wojcicki did a lot of revolutionary work and contributed to accessible care. Unfortunately,  things just fell through and by now, I’m not so sure if this company has anymore future success ahead of it.
